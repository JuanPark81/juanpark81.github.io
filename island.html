: Island

I worked on some of the early concept art for the TVING series `Island'. 
I designed creatures, pitched ideas for how `Van', the main character, might fight,
and helped design `Benjulle', the tree monster.
I was drawing a lot of gory stuff around that time and got a little carried away with some of the designs. Unsurprisingly (and thankfully), the most extreme ones didn’t make the cut. 
Just a heads-up before you scroll down: some of the sketches are pretty gruesome.
