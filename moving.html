: Moving

I worked briefly on the early concept art for `Moving', the Disney+ series based on Kang Full’s Korean webtoon. 
Most of my time on the project went into exploring how each character’s powers might look on screen.
I think I spent more time digging through visual references than I did drawing.
It was fun to see how those powers came across in the finished show.
One of the highlights for me was getting to read the scripts `Kang Full' wrote himself.
They were so good, episode after episode.
Here, I’ve put together some of my early sketches exploring how `Doo sik', one of the main characters,
might fly, alongside scenes from the show.

Directed by In-je Park, Seong-hun Kim
Written by Kang Full
