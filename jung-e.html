: Jung_E

I worked on some of the early concept art for the Netflix film Jung_E.
I explored ideas for Jung_E’s combat suit and gear in her human mercenary form and helped design her robot form as well. One part I spent a lot of time on was figuring out what she’d look like at each stage as she was taken apart in the lab.
I also explored how the trailers in the film’s world were built and what they looked like. I even designed some firearms for the enemy robots to carry, though those designs didn’t make it into the final film.

Directed by Sang-ho Yeon
