: Maple story X BewhY / Kain MV

Client · Nexon, Delpic / 2021 / Game cinematic MV

Written by Nexon
compose, lyrics by BewhY
Storyboard by JungHwan Park
I put together a 2D storyboard reel for Nexon’s Maple Story cinematic, a music video featuring famous Korean rapper BewhY. He was working on the track at the same time I was making the reel, and we had to wrap up at about the same time. The tricky part was figuring out the timing without hearing the finished track, while still getting everything Nexon wanted into the Music video.
I was nearly done with the reel when I got my first listen to a rough version of BewhY’s beat. 
It had a more laid-back feel than I’d imagined. 
A slightly faster tempo might have been a closer match for the visuals, but 
...I really like the track on its own.
