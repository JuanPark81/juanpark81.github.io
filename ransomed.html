: Ransomed

I worked on some of the early concept art for `Ransomed'. 
While the film was still at the script stage, I painted a few keyframes based on how I pictured the scenes as I read them. Another VFX studio took over for production, so for a while I didn’t hear much about how things were going.
Then, after the shoot, I got an unexpected chance to revisit the project.
I was asked to paint over the original plates to give the VFX team a visual guide.
That gave me an early look at how the footage compared with what I’d imagined. 
It was really rewarding to see how closely so many of the shots matched those early paintings.

Directed by Seong-hun Kim
