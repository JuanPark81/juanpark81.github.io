About Juan

Hi, I’m Jung Hwan Park, but I go by Juan. 
I’m an art director, storyboard artist, and concept artist based in South Korea. 
I’ve spent over 15 years working across film, television, animation, and game cinematics.
One of my favorite parts of the job is reading a script for the first time.
What does this world look like?
What’s everyday life like for the characters who live there?
Those questions are usually where I start sketching.
I enjoy staying with a project from those first sketches through the final shot.
I can help plan a project, explore ideas through concept art, and work out how a scene will play through storyboards. As production moves along, I’m just as comfortable directing a sequence, helping the team keep things on track, or guiding the final look in post.
I also teach 2D animation to university students and share what I’ve learned along the way.
If you have a project in mind or just want to say "hello",
I’d love to hear from you :)
