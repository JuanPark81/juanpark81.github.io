: GT pt.01

Client · 4th / 2015 / 2D Animatics

Directed by Jeon-hyoung Lee
Written by Jeon-hyoung Lee
Storyboard by JungHwan Park
I worked on this project more than 10 years ago. A shorter version did get made in 3D, though it went in a different direction from what I’d imagined. The longer version I had in mind never made it past development.
Could it still happen after all this time? I have a feeling it probably won’t, so I dug this out. 
It felt a bit like opening an old journal.
What you see here is an unfinished single-take animatic.
It covers exactly half of the sequence I originally planned. These days, I think of it as a personal study.
If I ever find the time, I’d love to come back to it and finish Part. 2 the way I pictured it back then.
