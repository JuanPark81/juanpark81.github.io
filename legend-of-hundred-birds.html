: Legend of Hundred Birds' Clothes / Preshow

Client · WANDA, China / 2016 / IMAX Dome screen attraction

Directed by JungHwan Park
Written by SangJong Shim
Storyboard by JungHwan Park
I first heard of 100 Birds Clothes, a legend story in Nánníng, China, when I started working on this project.
I storyboarded the pre-show for a theme park ride based on the legend. It’s a short film that introduces guests to the story before they board. I later got to work on the main ride film, too. I learned a lot from being involved in both storyboarding and production.
Working on this project helped me see where I still had room to grow and how I could get better. I also got to know some really great people along the way :)
